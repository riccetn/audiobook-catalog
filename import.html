Fill in series from Audible

Looks your books up on Audible by their ASIN, and fills in series and numbers they don't have, and how many books are out in series without release info. Nothing you have is changed, and you see what would change first.

Hardcover

Import adds the books on your Hardcover Read shelf, Export puts your books on it with their dates read, and Sync does both. Nothing is changed or removed on either side, and you see what would happen first.

Export to Goodreads

Downloads a CSV of every book for Goodreads' import page: on the read shelf, with the latest date read, genres as shelves, and the Goodreads id and ISBN of an edition so Goodreads picks that edition.

Backups

Export downloads the books, the series and author info and the list of books imports skip. Restore replaces the catalogue with a backup. Merge combines a backup from another device (your phone, say) with this catalogue when both have changed.
