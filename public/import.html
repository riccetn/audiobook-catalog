Backup

The catalog lives in this browser only, so export a backup now and then: it has the books, the series and author info and the list of books imports skip. Restore replaces the catalog with a backup, or with the data files of a catalog from before it lived in the browser (pick books.json, series-info.json, authors.json, excluded.txt and not-duplicates.txt together). Merge combines a backup from another device (your phone, say) with this catalog when both have changed.
